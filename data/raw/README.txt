MovieLens Latest Small Dataset
==============================

Source: GroupLens Research, University of Minnesota
Dataset page: https://grouplens.org/datasets/movielens/latest/
Direct download: https://files.grouplens.org/datasets/movielens/ml-latest-small.zip

Files used:
- movies.csv: movie identifiers, titles, and genres
- ratings.csv: user ratings and timestamps
- tags.csv: user-supplied movie tags
- links.csv: IMDb and TMDb identifiers (included but not required by the model)

MovieLens datasets are intended for education and research. Consult the
official dataset README and GroupLens terms before redistribution or commercial use.
